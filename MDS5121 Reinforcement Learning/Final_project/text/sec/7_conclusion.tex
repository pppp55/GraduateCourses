\section{Conclusion}

This survey reviewed the development of reinforcement learning based recommendation systems from early algorithmic adaptations to modern system-level architectures. We presented a structured taxonomy, discussed representative works, and summarized emerging trends that emphasize long-term value optimization, simulator-based training, and integration with large language models.

Overall, the field is undergoing a paradigm shift from short-term, single-step optimization toward holistic, long-horizon decision making. Reinforcement learning provides a principled framework for this transition, but its success in recommendation systems depends not only on algorithmic innovation, but also on advances in system design, evaluation methodology, and responsible deployment. We hope this survey serves as a useful reference and inspires future research in this rapidly evolving area.
